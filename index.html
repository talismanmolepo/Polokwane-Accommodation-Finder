About Us

Connecting Students with Quality Accommodation in Polokwane

Polokwane Student Living is the premier housing portal dedicated to providing verified, high-quality, and affordable student accommodation in Polokwane and surrounding areas in Limpopo.

Whether you are attending the University of Limpopo, Tshwane University of Technology (TUT) Polokwane Campus, Capricorn TVET College, or private institutes, our directory helps you find safe, comfortable rooms with transparent pricing.

  • 39 Hand-picked Accommodations: Ranging from single study rooms to spacious bachelors and sharing flats.
  • NSFAS Compliance: Easily filter properties that accept direct NSFAS funding and allowances.
  • Neighborhood Coverage: Find homes in Flora Park, Serala View, Bendor, Seshego, CBD, Eduan Park, and more.
